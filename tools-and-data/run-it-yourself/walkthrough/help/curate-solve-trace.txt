usage: fractal-wallpapers curate solve trace [-h] [--out DIR] [--bundle DIR] [stamp]

For each seat: its gallery.jsonl row, its candidate-ledger row, scores and flatness, its
pool_scores row, its place's supply_scores and locations.jsonl rows, and the walk.jsonl rows from
the place back to its lineage's root. Writes sliced copies of each file (only the rows on those
chains, byte for byte, plus one walk row of every other kind) under files/, counts.json with each
full file's row count, and a page (default artifacts/curation/trace/<stamp>/). Also follows three
of each reject: the lowest-scored places, candidates under the pool gate, and candidates that
cleared it and missed the fine bar. A chain that cannot be followed is listed with the file it
stopped at.

positional arguments:
  stamp         the record (default the newest)

options:
  -h, --help    show this help message and exit
  --out DIR     write the trace here
  --bundle DIR  also write the page into DIR as a portable folder: index.html, page.json, and only
                the pictures it shows, re-encoded small; nothing outside DIR is referred to
