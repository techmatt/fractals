Every place the search kept

One mark for every location the search has found and a judge has kept, on the parameter plane it came out of. A Julia location is a value of c, and c is a point of that same plane, so both halves of the search are drawn here together. Hover a mark and the three frames above the plane fill with the Julia set it stands for, its neighborhood on the plane, and the colored render a judge scored; click to keep one, and Escape to let it go. Each of the three opens in the explorer at the view it is, and says in its tooltip what the link cannot carry.
