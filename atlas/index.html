Fractal atlases

Every place the search kept

One mark for every place the search has kept, on the parameter plane it came out of. A place is on the plate because at least one render there scored above the bar a gallery solve seats over, and nothing else puts a mark here. Blue is a location on the parameter plane itself; red is a Julia location, which is a value of c, and c is a point of this same plane. Both halves of the search are drawn together, and a mark is one of them or the other and never both. Hover a mark and the three frames above the plane fill with its neighborhood on the plane, the Julia set it stands for, and a wallpaper drawn at it; click to keep one, and Escape to let it go. Each of the three opens in the explorer at the view it is, and says in its tooltip what the link cannot carry.
