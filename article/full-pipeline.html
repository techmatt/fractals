Section 10 of 12

Full pipeline

The project runs as two phases over one store. A harvest searches every fractal family for locations worth rendering and adds everything it admits to a pool that only grows; a curation pass then selects a gallery out of that whole pool, whichever run each picture in it came from, and renders the winners at wallpaper resolution. The sections before this one describe the parts one at a time. This section is about the shape they make together — what each phase is trying to achieve, and why the two are kept apart.
