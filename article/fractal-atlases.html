Fractal atlases

Every location the search has kept sits somewhere in one of the fractal families, and a family is a picture in its own right. This section maps them: each family drawn whole, with the locations the pipeline has found marked on it, so that where the good ground lies — and where the search has never brought anything back — can be looked at rather than tabulated. Each mark opens at the view it stands for.

The atlas itself is built: every place the search kept, drawn on the plane it came out of, with the renderer running in the browser. It is standing on a synthetic record until the search's own one lands, and it says so above the plot.
