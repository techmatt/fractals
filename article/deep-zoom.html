Deep zoom

Zoom far enough and two neighboring samples round to the same number, and the arithmetic an ordinary renderer runs in can no longer tell them apart. This section is about where that wall actually sits, what it takes to work right up against it, and what a picture drawn near it can and cannot be trusted to say.
